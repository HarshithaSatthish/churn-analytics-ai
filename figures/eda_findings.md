# EDA Findings

- O1: Month-to-month contracts churn at 42.7%, vs 11.3% (one-year) and 2.8% (two-year).

- O2: Customers in their first 6 months churn at 52.9%, falling to 14.0% after 25+ months — churn is a new-customer problem.

- O3: Churned customers paid $74.44/mo on average vs $61.27/mo for retained — higher bills correlate with churn.

- O4: Median tenure is 29 months; 31.0% of customers are within their first year.

- O5: Strongest linear associates of churn: is_month_to_month (|r|=0.41), tenure (|r|=0.35), is_fiber (|r|=0.31).

- O6: Fiber-optic customers churn at 41.9%.

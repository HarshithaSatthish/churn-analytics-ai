# EDA Findings

- O1: Month-to-month churn is 42.7% vs 11.3% (one-year) and 2.8% (two-year).

- O2: Churn falls from 52.9% in months 0-6 to 14.0% after 25+ months.

- O3: Churned customers average $74.44/month vs $61.27/month for retained customers.

- O4: Median tenure is 29 months; 31.0% of customers are within their first year.

- O5: Strongest linear associates with churn are is_month_to_month (|r|=0.41), tenure (|r|=0.35), and is_fiber (|r|=0.31).

- O6: Electronic-check customers churn at 45.3%.

- O7: Customers without tech support churn at 41.6% vs 15.2% with tech support.
